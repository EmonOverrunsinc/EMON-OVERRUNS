Delete Your E-Portal Account

EMON OVERRUNS E-PORTAL (website and apps for Android and Windows)

How to ask

  • In the app: sign in, open My Profile and press Request Account Deletion.
  • On this page: fill in the form below. You do not need to sign in.
  • By email: write to emonoverruns@gmail.com from the email address of your account.

We may reply to your email to make sure the request comes from you. Your account is deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • your login (email address, username and password);
  • your name, profile photo and signature;
  • your chat messages, community posts and notices;
  • your job application and its files, if it was not approved.

What is kept

Business records that Philippine tax, business and labour laws require us to keep, such as invoices, receipts, statements, payslips, employee records and order letters, are kept for as long as the law requires, then deleted. In those records your account is shown as Deleted User, and nobody can sign in with it.
