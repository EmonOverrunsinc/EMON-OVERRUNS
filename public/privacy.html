EMON OVERRUNS E-PORTAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how EMON OVERRUNS handles personal information in the EMON OVERRUNS E-PORTAL: the website www.emonoverruns.online and the E-Portal apps for Android and Windows. The E-Portal is the business system of EMON OVERRUNS for customer accounts, invoices, payments, statements, employees, job applications and record verification. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Who we are

EMON OVERRUNS, Pasay City, Metro Manila, Philippines.
Data protection contact: Emon Hasan, Managing Director, emonoverruns@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Your login account: name, email address, username, password (kept in coded form by our sign-in service; nobody at EMON OVERRUNS can read it), profile photo and signature if you upload them, your position and access level.
  • Customer accounts (entered by our staff): name, business name, address, phone number, email, Facebook name or link, photo, identity and requirement documents, invoices, payments, statements, credit memos and order letters.
  • Job applications: name, contact details, present and permanent address, date and place of birth, gender, religion, blood group, ID number, names of parents and spouse, education, work experience, photo and the salary you ask for.
  • Employees: employee details, payslips, salary and advance records, and orders.
  • Messages: chat messages and community posts inside the E-Portal, and messages sent with Contact Us (your name, how we should reply, and your message).
  • Files: documents, photos and scans that you or our staff upload. Pictures are made smaller before they are saved.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a code or take a photo for an upload. The E-Portal does not use your device's location.
  • Technical information: our hosting providers keep server logs (such as IP address, time and the page asked for) for security. We measure page loading speed with Vercel Speed Insights, which uses no cookies and does not identify you.

3. Why we use it

  • to keep customer accounts, invoices, payments and monthly statements, and to send statements to customers;
  • to check that a printed record is genuine (the Verify a record page);
  • to review job applications, and to manage employees and payroll;
  • to let you sign in, keep your account safe and stop misuse;
  • to answer your messages;
  • to follow Philippine tax, business and labour laws.

We do not sell personal information, we do not show advertisements, and we do not share your information for marketing.

4. Who can see it

  • Our staff, only for their work: each staff member sees only the parts of the E-Portal that the Director allows.
  • Anyone with a record's code or QR code can check on the Verify a record page that the record is genuine. That page shows only the record's main details.
  • Service providers who run the E-Portal for us: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in, with servers in Singapore), Vercel (website hosting) and Google (sending sign-in emails). They handle the information only to provide their service.
  • Government offices when the law requires it.

5. How we protect it

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Every user signs in, and the database itself checks what each person may see or change. Saved records cannot be changed without a trace: corrections are added as new records and approved by the Director.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your login account: until you ask us to delete it, or until it is no longer needed.
  • Business records (invoices, receipts, statements, payslips, order letters and their history): as long as Philippine tax and business laws require, then they are deleted.
  • A job application that was not approved: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Contact Us messages: until they are answered and no longer needed.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to see your information, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used. Write to emonoverruns@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

8. Deleting your account

You can ask to delete your E-Portal account in the app (My Profile › Request Account Deletion) or on the Delete Account page. We explain there what is deleted and what the law requires us to keep.

9. Children

The E-Portal is for business use by adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18 years old.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
